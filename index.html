      Reading your first tab

      Each horizontal line is a string. The top line is the highest-sounding string. A number tells you which fret to press. A zero means an open string.

      G |-------------|
D |-------------|
A |-----3-------|
E |-0-------3---|

      Read from left to right: play the open E string, fret 3 on the A string, then fret 3 on the E string.

      Press Play to hear the rhythm. Start at 50% speed, follow the highlighted notes, and repeat a few bars until they feel comfortable.

      Writing a song

      A song is plain text: a few header lines, then bars of notes separated by |.

      title: My groove
artist: Jane Doe, John Roe  # optional: who wrote it
about: Eighth notes on the root  # optional: what the song trains
listen: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...  # optional: the original
instrument: bass4      # bass4, bass5, bass6, guitar6
tuning: D2 A2 D3 G3    # optional: any tuning, low to high
tempo: 90
key: C                 # C, Ab, F#m, ...
time: 4/4

@Verse                 # section name
"C" :q 3.3 3.3 :8 5.2 5.2 :q 3.3 |
(3.3 5.2):h r:h |      # two notes together, then a rest
[3/4] :8t 3.3 5.3 7.3 :q ~7.3 r |
      artist:who wrote the song, shown as "by ...". Separate several names with commas
      about:one line on what the song trains, shown under the title
      level:easy, medium or hard. Put it under a == Name line to rate one version
      listen:web address of a recording of the original, shown as a "Listen to the original" link
      3.4fret 3 on string 4 (string 1 is the highest)
      :qduration for the next notes: w h q 8 16 32. Add . for dotted and t for triplet
      3.4:8duration for one note
      (3.4 5.3)notes played together
      rrest
      ~5.3tie: keep the note ringing without picking it again
      "Am7"chord name above the next note
      {let ring}text above the next note (dynamics, tips)
      <la la la>words to sing, below the tab from the next note
      [3/4]time signature from this bar on
      @Chorussection name, on its own line
      == Bass linestarts another version of the song, on its own line. Versions share the header. Switch between them with the buttons under the title
