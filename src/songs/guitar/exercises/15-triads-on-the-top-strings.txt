title: Triads on the Top Strings
artist: TabTrainer
about: Play three-note chords on the top three strings and find the same chord in three places on the neck.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 76
key: C
time: 4/4

# A triad is a chord of just three notes. On strings 3, 2 and 1 each chord has three shapes,
# one for each note that can sit on top. Learn them as small groups of frets you can move.

== Learn
level: medium
@C in four places
# The same notes C, E and G each time: open, then around frets 4, 8 and 12.
# That is three shapes: the one at fret 12 is the open shape an octave higher.
"C" :q 0.3 1.2 0.1 (0.3 1.2 0.1) | 5.3 5.2 3.1 (5.3 5.2 3.1) |
9.3 8.2 8.1 (9.3 8.2 8.1) | 12.3 13.2 12.1 (12.3 13.2 12.1) |

== Develop
level: medium
@C, F and G close together
# Three chords between frets 3 and 6. Each finger moves three frets or less.
"C" :8 5.3 5.2 3.1 5.2 :q (5.3 5.2 3.1) (5.3 5.2 3.1) | "F" :8 5.3 6.2 5.1 6.2 :q (5.3 6.2 5.1) (5.3 6.2 5.1) |
"G" :8 4.3 3.2 3.1 3.2 :q (4.3 3.2 3.1) (4.3 3.2 3.1) | "C" :8 5.3 5.2 3.1 5.2 :q (5.3 5.2 3.1) (5.3 5.2 3.1) |

== Challenge
level: hard
@Up the scale
# One chord on each of the first six notes of the C major scale, climbing the neck and coming back.
"C" :q (5.3 5.2 3.1) "Dm" (7.3 6.2 5.1) "Em" (9.3 8.2 7.1) "F" (10.3 10.2 8.1) |
"G" (12.3 12.2 10.1) "Am" :h (14.3 13.2 12.1) "G" :q (12.3 12.2 10.1) |
"F" (10.3 10.2 8.1) "Em" (9.3 8.2 7.1) "Dm" :h (7.3 6.2 5.1) |
"C" (5.3 5.2 3.1) r |
@Smallest moves
# Keep every note that the next chord shares and move the others to the nearest fret.
"C" :q (5.3 5.2 3.1) (5.3 5.2 3.1) "Am" (5.3 5.2 5.1) (5.3 5.2 5.1) | "F" (5.3 6.2 5.1) (5.3 6.2 5.1) "G" (4.3 3.2 3.1) (4.3 3.2 3.1) |
"C" :q. (5.3 5.2 3.1) :8 (5.3 5.2 3.1) "Am" :h (5.3 5.2 5.1) | "F" :q. (5.3 6.2 5.1) :8 (5.3 6.2 5.1) "G" :h (4.3 3.2 3.1) |
@Off-beat hits
# Count "1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and": the chord sounds on every "and", never on the number.
"C" :8 r (5.3 5.2 3.1) r (5.3 5.2 3.1) r (5.3 5.2 3.1) r (5.3 5.2 3.1) | "Am" r (5.3 5.2 5.1) r (5.3 5.2 5.1) r (5.3 5.2 5.1) r (5.3 5.2 5.1) |
"F" r (5.3 6.2 5.1) r (5.3 6.2 5.1) r (5.3 6.2 5.1) r (5.3 6.2 5.1) | "G" r (4.3 3.2 3.1) r (4.3 3.2 3.1) r (4.3 3.2 3.1) r (4.3 3.2 3.1) |
@Broken chords
# Each half bar is one shape picked low, middle, high, middle. The hand climbs one shape at a time, then comes back down.
"C" :8 0.3 1.2 0.1 1.2 5.3 5.2 3.1 5.2 | 9.3 8.2 8.1 8.2 12.3 13.2 12.1 13.2 |
"F" 10.3 10.2 8.1 10.2 "G" 12.3 12.2 10.1 12.2 | 7.3 8.2 7.1 8.2 "C" :h (5.3 5.2 3.1) |
