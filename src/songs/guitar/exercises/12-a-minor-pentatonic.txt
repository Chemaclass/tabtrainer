title: A Minor Pentatonic
artist: TabTrainer
about: The five-note scale behind most rock and blues solos, in its most common shape at the fifth fret, then a short lick built from it and the move to the next shape at the seventh fret.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 80
key: Am
time: 4/4

# The notes are A, C, D, E and G. Index finger on fret 5, ring finger on fret 7, little finger on fret 8. Pick down, up, down, up all the way through.
