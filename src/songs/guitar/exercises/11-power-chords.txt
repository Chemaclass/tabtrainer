title: Power Chords
artist: TabTrainer
about: Move one two-finger shape along the low strings to play power chords, then add stops and quick changes.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 84
key: Em
time: 4/4

# A power chord is a root plus the fifth note of its scale. Index finger on the root, ring finger two frets higher on the next thinner string. E5 uses the open low string, so one finger is enough. Strike only the strings in the shape.
# Two marks come in Challenge. _ before a chord or note is a palm mute: rest the edge of your picking hand on the strings where they leave the bridge, so it sounds short and thick. x is a dead strum: keep your fingers on the strings without pressing, and strum, so you hear a click and no note.

level: medium
