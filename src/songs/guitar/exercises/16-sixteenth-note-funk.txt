title: Sixteenth Note Funk
artist: TabTrainer
about: Keep your strumming hand moving in sixteenth notes and place short chord hits exactly on the parts of the beat.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 80
key: Am
time: 4/4

# Move your strumming hand down and up on every sixteenth note: down on the beat and the "and", up on the "e" and the "a".
# In Learn, a rest is a stroke that misses the strings. Keep the hand moving and let go of the chord a little so each hit stays short.
# From Develop on, the strokes between the hits become dead strums, written x: keep your fingers on the chord shape but stop pressing, so the strings click with no note. Press down only for the hits.
# Am7 and D9: lay your ring finger flat across strings 1 to 3 at fret 5. For Am7 your middle finger takes fret 5 on string 4.
# For D9 lift it and put your index on fret 4 of string 4. D9 leaves out its lowest note, D; a bass player would play it.
