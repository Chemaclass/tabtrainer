title: Pentatonic Across the Neck
artist: TabTrainer
about: Join the five shapes of A minor pentatonic so you can play the scale anywhere on the neck.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 80
key: Am
time: 4/4

# Every note is A, C, D, E or G. Each shape fits under one hand position; the rests give you time to move.
# Shape 1 starts at fret 5, shape 2 at fret 7, shape 3 at fret 9 or 10, shape 4 at fret 12, shape 5 at fret 2 (and again at 14).
== Learn
level: medium
# Shape 1: index on fret 5. Shape 2: index on fret 7, little finger on fret 10.
@Shape 1 up, shape 2 down
"Am" :8 5.6 8.6 5.5 7.5 5.4 7.4 5.3 7.3 | 5.2 8.2 5.1 8.1 :q r :8 10.1 8.1 |
10.2 8.2 9.3 7.3 10.4 7.4 10.5 7.5 | 10.6 8.6 7.5 10.5 :h 7.4 |

== Develop
level: hard
# Shape 3: index on fret 10, but reach back to fret 9 on string 3. Shape 4: index on fret 12, little finger on fret 15.
@Shapes 3 and 4
"Am" :8 10.6 12.6 10.5 12.5 10.4 12.4 9.3 12.3 | 10.2 13.2 10.1 12.1 :q r :8 15.1 12.1 |
15.2 13.2 14.3 12.3 14.4 12.4 15.5 12.5 | 15.6 12.6 :q 12.5 :h r |
# Shape 5: index on fret 2, little finger on fret 5. Then shift back to shape 1.
@Shape 5
:8 3.6 5.6 3.5 5.5 2.4 5.4 2.3 5.3 | 3.2 5.2 3.1 5.1 :q r :8 8.1 5.1 |
8.2 5.2 7.3 5.3 7.4 5.4 7.5 5.5 | 8.6 5.6 :h. 5.6 |

== Challenge
level: hard
# Two notes per string, one shape at a time. Slide your whole hand up every four notes.
@Along two strings
"Am" :8 3.2 5.2 3.1 5.1 5.2 8.2 5.1 8.1 | 8.2 10.2 8.1 10.1 10.2 13.2 10.1 12.1 |
13.2 15.2 12.1 15.1 12.1 10.1 13.2 10.2 | 10.1 8.1 10.2 8.2 :q 8.1 5.1 |
# Three notes per string on strings 6 to 3, then two on strings 2 and 1. Move your hand up a little on each new string.
# Use index, middle and little finger where the wider gap comes second (strings 6 and 5), index, ring and little finger where it comes first (strings 4 and 3).
@Diagonal run
:8t 3.6 5.6 8.6 5.5 7.5 10.5 7.4 10.4 12.4 9.3 12.3 14.3 | 13.2 15.2 12.1 15.1 12.1 15.2 13.2 14.3 12.3 :q 12.5 |
:8t 14.3 12.3 9.3 12.4 10.4 7.4 10.5 7.5 5.5 8.6 5.6 3.6 | :q 5.6 3.6 :h 5.6 |
