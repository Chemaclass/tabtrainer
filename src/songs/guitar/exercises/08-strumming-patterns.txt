title: Strumming Patterns
artist: TabTrainer
about: Strum G, Em, C and D with a steady down-up hand, from a basic down-up pattern to one that skips a beat, a gallop and early chord changes.
level: easy
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 76
key: G
time: 4/4

# A full chord is a down strum ({d}): sweep from the low strings to the high ones.
# Three notes on the top strings are an up strum ({u}): catch just the thin strings on the way back.
# Keep the hand moving down and up in eighth notes even when it does not touch the strings. A chord with every note tied (~) is a stroke that misses: the last strum keeps ringing.

== Learn
level: easy
@Down, down, down-up, down
"G" :q {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) :8 {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) {u} (0.3 0.2 3.1) :q {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) | "Em" :q (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) :8 (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) (0.3 0.2 0.1) :q (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) |
"C" :q (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) :8 (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) (0.3 1.2 0.1) :q (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) | "D" :q (0.4 2.3 3.2 2.1) (0.4 2.3 3.2 2.1) :8 (0.4 2.3 3.2 2.1) (2.3 3.2 2.1) :q (0.4 2.3 3.2 2.1) |

== Develop
level: medium
@Skip the third beat
# The tied chord on beat 3 is a missed down strum: the hand moves down past the strings without hitting them, then comes back up.
"G" :q {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) :8 {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) {u} (0.3 0.2 3.1) (~0.3 ~0.2 ~3.1) {u} (0.3 0.2 3.1) {d} (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) {u} (0.3 0.2 3.1) | "Em" :q (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) :8 (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) (0.3 0.2 0.1) (~0.3 ~0.2 ~0.1) (0.3 0.2 0.1) (0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 0.2 0.1) (0.3 0.2 0.1) |
