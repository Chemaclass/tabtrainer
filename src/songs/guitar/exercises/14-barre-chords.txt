title: Barre Chords
artist: TabTrainer
about: Build the barre step by step, from a small F on four strings to full shapes you can slide to any fret.
level: medium
instrument: guitar6
tempo: 66
key: C
time: 4/4

# A barre is your first finger pressed flat across several strings at one fret.
# Roll it slightly onto its bony side and keep your thumb behind the neck.

== Learn
level: medium
@Small F
# First finger flat across strings 1 and 2 at fret 1, second finger on string 3 at fret 2, third finger on string 4 at fret 3.
"F" :h (3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) r | "C" (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) r | "F" (3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) r | "G" (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) r |

== Develop
level: medium
@Full barre
# One shape with the root on string 6. Slide it to fret 3 for G during the rest.
# At fret 5, lift the finger on string 3 and the barre turns the chord into A minor.
"F" :q (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) :h r | "G" :q (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) :h r |
"Am" :q (5.6 7.5 7.4 5.3 5.2 5.1) (5.6 7.5 7.4 5.3 5.2 5.1) :h r | "G" :q (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) :h r |

== Challenge
level: hard
@Major and minor
# Lift the finger on string 3 and the barre plays that string: major turns minor.
"F" :q (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) "Fm" (1.6 3.5 3.4 1.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 1.3 1.2 1.1) |
"G" (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) (3.6 5.5 5.4 4.3 3.2 3.1) "Gm" (3.6 5.5 5.4 3.3 3.2 3.1) (3.6 5.5 5.4 3.3 3.2 3.1) |
"F" :8 (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 2.3 1.2 1.1) "Fm" (1.6 3.5 3.4 1.3 1.2 1.1) (1.6 3.5 3.4 1.3 1.2 1.1) :q (1.6 3.5 3.4 1.3 1.2 1.1) |

@Root on the A string
# Now the root is on string 5. Leave string 6 silent: touch it lightly with the tip of your first finger.
"Bm" :q (2.5 4.4 4.3 3.2 2.1) (2.5 4.4 4.3 3.2 2.1) (2.5 4.4 4.3 3.2 2.1) r | "C" (3.5 5.4 5.3 5.2 3.1) (3.5 5.4 5.3 5.2 3.1) (3.5 5.4 5.3 5.2 3.1) r |
"D" (5.5 7.4 7.3 7.2 5.1) (5.5 7.4 7.3 7.2 5.1) (5.5 7.4 7.3 7.2 5.1) r | "C" (3.5 5.4 5.3 5.2 3.1) (3.5 5.4 5.3 5.2 3.1) "Bm" :h (2.5 4.4 4.3 3.2 2.1) |

@With open chords
# The strum from Strumming Patterns. A full chord is a down strum, three strings are an up strum.
# On beat 3 the hand moves down past the strings without hitting them: the tied chord keeps ringing.
"C" {d} :q (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) {d} :8 (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) {u} (0.3 1.2 0.1) (~0.3 ~1.2 ~0.1) {u} (0.3 1.2 0.1) {d} (3.5 2.4 0.3 1.2 0.1) {u} (0.3 1.2 0.1) |
"G" :q (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) :8 (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) (0.3 0.2 3.1) (~0.3 ~0.2 ~3.1) (0.3 0.2 3.1) (3.6 2.5 0.4 0.3 0.2 3.1) (0.3 0.2 3.1) |
