title: Chromatic Walk in C
artist: BassBuzz
about: Walk up and down in C with chromatic passing notes, each leading a fret into the next scale note, from the BassBuzz daily practice video.
level: medium
listen: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=riCDOFyRIZ4
instrument: bass4
tempo: 80
key: C
time: 4/4

# Keep one finger per fret from fret 2. Shift up one fret for the Ab (6.2), then back for the E (2.2).
== Learn
level: medium
@Up from C
:q 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 | 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 |
@Back down to D
:q 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 | 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 |
@Home
:w 3.3 |

== Develop
level: medium
@Up from C
:q 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 | 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 |
@Back down to D
:q 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 | 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 |
@Push the passing notes
:q 3.3 :8 2.2 3.2 :q 4.2 5.2 | :q 2.1 :8 3.1 4.1 :q 5.1 3.1 |
:q 2.1 :8 6.2 5.2 :q 3.2 2.2 | :q 5.3 :8 2.2 1.2 :h 5.3 |
:w 3.3 |

== Challenge
level: hard
@Eighths up and down
:8 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 | 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 |
:8 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 | 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 |
@Down first, then up
:8 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 | 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 |
:8 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 5.2 3.2 2.2 5.3 | 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 |
@Push the passing notes
:q 3.3 :8 2.2 3.2 :q 4.2 5.2 | :q 2.1 :8 3.1 4.1 :q 5.1 3.1 |
:q 2.1 :8 6.2 5.2 :q 3.2 2.2 | :h 5.3 3.3 |
@Chromatic finish
:8 3.3 2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 2.1 3.1 4.1 | 5.1 3.1 2.1 6.2 5.2 3.2 2.2 1.2 |
:q 5.3 :8 2.2 1.2 :h 5.3 | :w 3.3 |
