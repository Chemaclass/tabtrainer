title: Latin Tumbao
artist: TabTrainer
about: The salsa bass pattern. You play just before beat 3 and on beat 4, and the beat 4 note is the next chord's root, held over the bar line. It trains anticipation and ties.
level: medium
instrument: bass4
tempo: 96
key: Am
time: 4/4

# Don't play on beat 1: you are still holding the note from the bar before.
== Learn
level: medium
@Montuno
"Am" :q r :8 r 7.3 :q ~7.3 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :q ~7.2 5.4 |
"Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :q ~7.3 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :q ~7.2 5.4 |
"Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :q ~7.3 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :q ~7.2 5.4 |
@Ending
"Am" :q ~5.4 r :h r |

== Develop
level: medium
# A third chord, E7. Its root is the same E you play in the Am bar, so stay at frets 5 to 7. The last two bars add the root an octave up on the "and" of beat 3.
@Montuno with E7
"Am" :q r :8 r 7.3 :q ~7.3 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :q ~7.2 7.3 |
"E7" :q ~7.3 :8 r 7.4 :q ~7.4 5.4 | "Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :q ~7.3 5.3 |
"D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :q ~7.2 7.3 | "E7" :q ~7.3 :8 r 7.4 :q ~7.4 5.4 |
"Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :8 ~7.3 7.2 :q 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :8 ~7.2 7.1 :q 5.4 |
@Ending
"Am" :q ~5.4 r :h r |

== Challenge
level: hard
# Each bar adds the root an octave up on the "and" of beat 3. Then every note comes a sixteenth earlier, so the next root lands before beat 4.
@Add the and of 3
"Am" :q r :8 r 7.3 :8 ~7.3 7.2 :q 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :8 ~7.2 7.1 :q 7.3 |
"E7" :q ~7.3 :8 r 7.4 :8 ~7.4 7.3 :q 5.4 | "Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :8 ~7.3 7.2 :q 5.3 |
"D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :8 ~7.2 7.1 :q 7.3 | "E7" :q ~7.3 :8 r 7.4 :8 ~7.4 7.3 :q 5.4 |
"Am" :q ~5.4 :8 r 7.3 :8 ~7.3 7.2 :q 5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :8 r 7.2 :8 ~7.2 7.1 :q 5.4 |
@Sixteenth anticipations
"Am" :q ~5.4 :16 r :q 7.3 :8 7.2 :16 5.3 :q ~5.3 | "D7" :q ~5.3 :16 r :q 7.2 :8 7.1 :16 7.3 :q ~7.3 |
"E7" :q ~7.3 :16 r :q 7.4 :8 7.3 :16 5.4 :q ~5.4 | "Am" :q ~5.4 :16 r :q 7.3 :8 7.2 :16 5.3 :q ~5.3 |
"D7" :q ~5.3 :16 r :q 7.2 :8 7.1 :16 7.3 :q ~7.3 | "E7" :q ~7.3 :16 r :q 7.4 :8 7.3 :16 5.4 :q ~5.4 |
"Am" :q ~5.4 :16 r :q 7.3 :8 7.2 :16 5.4 :q ~5.4 | :q ~5.4 :16 r 8.4 5.3 7.3 5.2 7.2 5.1 7.1 :8 7.2 5.4 |
@Ending
"Am" :q ~5.4 r :h r |
