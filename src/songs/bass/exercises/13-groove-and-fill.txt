title: Groove and Fill
artist: TabTrainer
about: Hold a groove through four chords, play a one-bar fill on the last one and land back on the first beat.
level: medium
instrument: bass4
tempo: 88
key: C
time: 4/4

# Index finger on fret 2. Each chord is its root and fifth, and every fill walks from G back to C.
== Learn
level: medium
@Groove
"C" :q 3.3 3.3 5.2 3.3 | "Am" 5.4 5.4 2.2 5.4 | "F" 3.2 3.2 5.1 3.2 | "G" 3.4 3.4 5.3 3.4 |
"C" 3.3 3.3 5.2 3.3 | "Am" 5.4 5.4 2.2 5.4 | "F" 3.2 3.2 5.1 3.2 |
@Fill
"G" 3.4 2.3 5.3 2.3 |
@Return
"C" :q 3.3 3.3 :h 3.3 |
