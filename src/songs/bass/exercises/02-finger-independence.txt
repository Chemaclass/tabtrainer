title: Finger Independence
artist: TabTrainer
about: Give each fretting finger its own turn in a chromatic pattern across the strings.
level: easy
instrument: bass4
tempo: 72
key: C
time: 4/4

# This chromatic exercise includes notes outside the C major scale.
# Place your index finger at fret 5 and your pinky at fret 8.
== Learn
level: easy
@One finger per fret
:q 5.4 6.4 7.4 8.4 | 5.3 6.3 7.3 8.3 |
5.2 6.2 7.2 8.2 | 5.1 6.1 7.1 8.1 |
:w 8.4 |

== Develop
level: medium
@Reverse the pattern
:8 5.4 6.4 7.4 8.4 8.4 7.4 6.4 5.4 | 5.3 6.3 7.3 8.3 8.3 7.3 6.3 5.3 |
5.2 6.2 7.2 8.2 8.2 7.2 6.2 5.2 | 5.1 6.1 7.1 8.1 8.1 7.1 6.1 5.1 |
