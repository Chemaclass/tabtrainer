title: Finger Independence
artist: TabTrainer
about: Give each fretting finger its own turn in a chromatic pattern across the strings.
level: easy
instrument: bass4
tempo: 72
key: C
time: 4/4

# This chromatic exercise includes notes outside the C major scale.
# Place your index finger at fret 2 and your pinky at fret 5.
== Learn
level: easy
@One finger per fret
:q 2.4 3.4 4.4 5.4 | 2.3 3.3 4.3 5.3 |
2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 | 2.1 3.1 4.1 5.1 |

== Develop
level: medium
@Reverse the pattern
:8 2.4 3.4 4.4 5.4 5.4 4.4 3.4 2.4 | 2.3 3.3 4.3 5.3 5.3 4.3 3.3 2.3 |
2.2 3.2 4.2 5.2 5.2 4.2 3.2 2.2 | 2.1 3.1 4.1 5.1 5.1 4.1 3.1 2.1 |

== Challenge
level: hard
@Change finger order
:8 2.4 4.4 3.4 5.4 5.4 3.4 4.4 2.4 | 2.3 4.3 3.3 5.3 5.3 3.3 4.3 2.3 |
2.2 4.2 3.2 5.2 5.2 3.2 4.2 2.2 | 2.1 4.1 3.1 5.1 5.1 3.1 4.1 2.1 |
